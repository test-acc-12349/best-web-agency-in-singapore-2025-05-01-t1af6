Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a custom website?

Typically, we complete projects within 4-6 weeks. Simple websites may take 2-3 weeks, while more complex projects with custom functionality might require 8-12 weeks. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

What ongoing support do you provide?

We offer comprehensive maintenance packages including regular updates, security monitoring, performance optimization, and 24/7 emergency support. Our team is always available to help with content updates and technical issues.

How do you ensure website security?

We implement multiple layers of security including SSL certificates, regular security audits, malware scanning, and automated backups. Our hosting partners provide additional DDoS protection and firewall services.

What's included in your SEO optimization?

Our SEO package includes keyword research, on-page optimization, meta tag optimization, schema markup, mobile optimization, and performance tuning. We also provide monthly reports tracking your search rankings and traffic.

Do you offer custom design services?

Yes, we create fully custom designs tailored to your brand and business goals. Our design process includes wireframing, mockups, and multiple revision rounds to ensure your complete satisfaction.
